Stratified K(π, 1)-schemes

When can the stratified fundamental group scheme recover the cohomology of a stratified bundle?

Smooth projective curves

For a smooth projective connected curve over k, our comparison theorem establishes the stratified K(π, 1)-condition. In particular, for every finite-dimensional module V, the group scheme cohomology vanishes in degrees n ≥ 2.

Cohomology of the stratified fundamental group of curves

Vo Quoc Bao, Phung Ho Hai, and Dao Van Thinh.

Preprint. arXiv:2507.20020, revised 13 September 2026.
